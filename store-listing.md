# 商店文案

Chrome、Edge、Firefox 使用以下英文文案。短描述同步自 `manifest.build.json`；两项功能名称统一为 **Table of Contents** 与 **Outline**。

## 编写约束

- 用自然句子说明用户能做什么，保留必要的功能名称。旧的 Chapter View 对应 Outline；目录功能使用 Table of Contents。
- Chrome 描述不罗列全部支持平台、不添加关键词段落。确需说明兼容性时，只提及与上下文直接相关的少量品牌，最多 5 个；完整列表通过官网提供。
- 同一关键词自然使用，通常少于 5 次；不要为搜索排名重复词语、品牌或同义名称。
- 2026-11-06 的拒审通知将 11 个平台名称的连续名单判为 Keyword Spam（Yellow Argon）。本版删除整段名单，以功能说明代替。
- 本轮后台修改仅保存草稿，不提交审核、不发布、不上传新扩展包；后续提交须遵循用户当次的明确指令。
- 保存后重新打开编辑页确认内容；后台已保存、已提交审核与公开生效分别报告。

规则来源：[Chrome Web Store Spam policy FAQ — Keyword spam](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/spam-faq#keyword-spam)。

## Short description

```text
Navigate long AI chats with a Table of Contents and Outline. Find sections, jump to the source, and read with less scrolling.
```

## Detailed description

```text
Polaris makes long AI conversations easier to navigate and read.

Table of Contents shows the structure of a conversation. Browse questions, headings, lists, and tables, then select an entry to jump to its source.

Outline provides a focused reading view. Expand sections, search for the part you need, and copy a section or the full response.

Light and dark modes keep the interface comfortable alongside your conversation. Available content depends on what the supported website makes accessible.

Your conversation content is processed locally in your browser. Polaris does not send it to the developer or third-party servers.
```
